SpaceGrotesk-Medium.woff2 (weight 500) and SpaceGrotesk-Bold.woff2 (weight 700)
are the official static webfonts, subset to Latin, Latin-1, general punctuation
(U+2010-U+2027) and arrows (U+2190-U+21FF) for this site.

SpaceGrotesk-SemiBold.woff2 (weight 600) is a static instance of the official
variable font SpaceGrotesk[wght].ttf at wght=600. The 2.0.0 release does not
publish a separate SemiBold file. It is subset the same way. The upstream
license does not declare a Reserved Font Name.
